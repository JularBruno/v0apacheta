## How this relates to `docs/design/`

`docs/design/` is the visual/product side (persona Marcos, tokens, landing and onboarding
briefs). This folder is the **message** side and is upstream of it: when they disagree,
this folder wins and `docs/design/` gets updated. Known disagreements are listed in
[trail-naming.md](trail-naming.md) and [brand-guideline.md § Open items](brand-guideline.md#open-items).

## The idea in one paragraph

Apacheta is **a curated compilation of methods for handling money and building wealth**,
benchmarked from the best sources (kept in the internal `methods/` notes, never in public
copy) and **reworked for Argentina**. The compilation is not a reading list: it is
turned into **a path of cairns (apachetas)**, where each cairn is a real action in the app.
The map *is* the method.
