## What it teaches (paraphrased)

The course is a path of seven stations, each a set of exercises you fill in and rewrite as you
learn:

1. **Purpose first.** Personal mission, brand purpose, a 5-year vision and three yearly goals,
   then check that they point the same way.
2. **The brand as a story.** The customer is the hero with a wish and a problem; the brand is the
   guide that hands over a plan and a call to action; the story shows what is won, what is
   avoided and how the hero changes. Plus values and the brand described as a person.
3. **One ideal customer, described in detail:** data, habits, goals, values, pains, where they
   search and chat, objections, a day in their life. Then a value proposition **validated** with
   that market.
4. **Content by goal and format.** A grid of educate / entertain / inform / inspire × text /
   video / image / audio, and five kinds of posts (behind the scenes, the moment of use, results
   and stories, inspiration, education). One piece of material is cut into many.
5. **Follow the decision:** how people notice the need, search and compare, buy and use, and judge
   afterwards; where they leave opinions.
6. **Funnel and paid amplification:** awareness → consideration → purchase, segmentation by
   direct and indirect interests, ad budgets, sales copy and persuasion biases (urgency,
   scarcity, price anchoring).
7. **Measure and adjust:** KPIs per funnel stage, each goal turned into a **hypothesis and a
   strategy**, a post-sale follow-up, and an imagined day one year from now.

### Proposal A — addition to 2.5 *Cerrá el mes* (no new step)

- **Lesson:** a budget is a guess. Each month you test it: "si bajo X, sobra Y". The close tells
  you if the guess held.
- **Action in the app:** at the close, write the one change for next month as a guess with its
  expected effect (same field the step already plans: "un cambio para el próximo").
- **Signal:** next month's close shows whether the guess moved the number, up or not.
- **Change course:** two guesses in a row that don't move it → change the lever (ingreso, gasto
  fijo, deuda), as 2.5 already says. Nothing about the person.

### Proposal B — addition to 5.1 (parked until chapter 5 is designed)

- **Lesson:** what you build needs a "para qué" of your own. Picture a normal day a year from now.
- **Action in the app:** ⚠️ none exists. Could be a goal in Seguidor de ahorro with a written
  reason. Product check.
- **Signal:** the goal has a reason and a date.
- **Change course:** if the picture changes, the goal changes too. That's not failing.

## Argentine rework

- The workbook is written in tú with a few vos slips. Anything we take is rewritten in vos.
- The examples (ad budget in USD, discounts, marketplaces) assume a business that sells.
  Apacheta's "sale" is a free sign-up and a habit, so funnel language translates to: someone
  hears of us → reads a note or uses a tool → starts the trail → comes back next month.
- Where Marcos actually is: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and WhatsApp groups, Google searches like
  "cómo salir de deudas". To confirm with real people, not assume.

## What we do NOT copy

- The name "Método Ecodiem", the "estaciones" names, the storytelling element list as a numbered
  framework, the "social commerce quadrant" and any wording from the workbook.
- Urgency, scarcity, price anchoring and discount copy.
- Growth goals as the brand's promise ("duplicar…", "+50 %").
- The closing "tag me" call-to-action style.
