# Brand workbook — Apacheta

**Internal working doc, not public copy.** The method's exercises, filled in for Apacheta, station
by station. Structure taken from the marketing workbook in
[methods/ecodiem-marketing.md](methods/ecodiem-marketing.md); the prompts are kept so we know what
each answer responds to. Once a station is agreed, its result moves into
[brand-guideline.md](brand-guideline.md) / [audiences.md](audiences.md) / [channels.md](channels.md).

How each exercise reads:
- **Prompt:** the workbook's exercise.
- **Bruno (original):** Bruno's earlier answer, verbatim. Written before D1–D17 (tú, crypto, a
  stable coin). Kept for the record, not as copy.
- **Review:** marketing read: what works, what clashes with [decisions.md](decisions.md).
- **v2 / v3:** the proposal to react to. Copy in vos.

Status: stations 1–2 v3 (Bruno's corrections of 2026-10-25), stations 3–4 v1 for review,
stations 5–7 pending.

## Frame for every station (decided 2026-10-25)

- **Now:** the **academy** (the map as a method), the **community**, and **donations** to grow
  both (D15). The coin and a business account/stock manager are future forks; no copy mentions
  them.
- **Marketing is the street first** (D16): stickers, real apachetas, pasacalles, pasquines.
- **Tone of the map** (D17): a method built on financial principles, in order, adapted to
  Argentina, that **leads to building wealth**. Said with conviction, without amounts or
  timelines. **There is no fixed time: it can take years**, and we say so.

### How we say "wealth" without promising

| Say | Don't say | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Construí patrimonio. | Hacete rico. | Patrimonio is concrete (what you own minus what you owe) and the app measures it. |
| El camino termina en la cumbre: patrimonio que trabaja para vos. | Libertad financiera en X años. | Direction, not a deadline. |
| No hay apuro. Puede llevar meses o años; lo que importa es no dejar de poner piedras. | Resultados en 30 días. | Honest about time; it's also the brand's personality. |
| Primero dejás de perder plata, después la guardás, después la hacés crecer. | Multiplicá tu plata. | Names the sequence the map actually teaches. |
| Principios simples, en el orden correcto. | El secreto que nadie te cuenta. | Anti-humo: nothing secret, just order. |

### 1.6 Personalidad y storytelling: el camino del héroe

**Prompt:** Describe brevemente los 8 elementos del Storytelling aplicándolos a tu marca: el héroe
y su deseo, el problema, el guía, el plan, llamada a la acción, la victoria, evitar el fracaso, la
transformación del héroe.

**Bruno (original):** answered element by element in the earlier paste (kept in git history).
Bruno, 2026-10-25: don't stick to what I wrote in each one; build the hero's path the way it's
done best. Mention there's no fixed time; it can take years.

**How a brand story is built well (the rules this version follows):**
1. **The person is the hero, never the brand.** Apacheta is the guide. If the brand is the one who
   wins, the story is an ad.
2. **One clear want**, concrete enough to picture. Not "mejorar sus finanzas".
3. **The problem has a villain and three layers:** what happens outside (external), how it feels
   (internal), and why it's unfair (philosophical). People buy the solution to the internal one.
4. **The guide shows empathy and authority** in one breath each: "te entiendo" and "sé el camino".
5. **The plan has two parts:** the steps (process) and the promises that remove fear (agreement).
6. **Two calls to action:** a direct one, and a lighter one for whoever isn't ready yet.
7. **Stakes and success are concrete images**, not adjectives.
8. **The transformation is an identity:** who the person becomes, said in their words.

On top of that, our story is literally a trail, so it also follows the classic **hero's journey**
(departure, trials, return). That gives every stretch of the map a place in the story (1.6b).

#### 1.6a The brand story, element by element (v3)

| Element | v3 | Why this way |
|---|---|---|
| **El héroe** | Alguien que ya se banca solo: labura, paga sus cosas y lleva su vida. Pero la plata le pasa por encima: no sabe bien cuánto gastó, y planear más allá del mes es adivinar. | Bruno's correction (2.2): self-supporting, can't plan ahead. |

> Te bancás solo, pagás tus cosas, y aun así la plata te pasa por encima: cuotas que no ves,

#### 1.6b The hero's journey on the trail

Every stretch of the map is a moment of the classic journey. This is the backbone for the
Cuadernito, the posts and the street pieces: each piece tells one moment.

| Journey moment | On the trail | What the person lives |
|---|---|---|
| El mundo de siempre | Antes de empezar | El mes a ciegas. Llega el resumen, sorpresa, se arregla como se puede. |
| El llamado | Un sticker, una deuda que aparece, las ganas de mudarse | Algo hace que planear deje de ser opcional. |
| La negativa | — | "No tengo tiempo", "no tengo plata para ahorrar", "otra app más". |
| El encuentro con el guía | Tu primera piedra | El baqueano le muestra el camino entero y le dice que no hay apuro. |
| Cruzar el umbral | Al pie del camino: traé tu último mes | Por primera vez ve sus números de verdad. No hay vuelta atrás: ya sabe. |
| Pruebas y aliados | La huella: vivir un mes con presupuesto | Meses que salen bien y meses que no. La comunidad acompaña. |
| La prueba más dura | El vado (con deudas) o el primer mes malo | Mirar la deuda de frente y cruzarla de a una. O un imprevisto que pone todo a prueba. |
| La recompensa | La aguada: la cantimplora llena | El primer colchón. Un imprevisto ya no lo manda a cero. |
| El camino largo | La cuesta | Invertir de a poco, durante años. Caídas, paciencia, seguir. Acá vive el "puede llevar años". |
| La cumbre | Construí lo que dura | Mira para atrás y ve el camino hecho. Desde arriba, elige qué construir. |
| El regreso con el regalo | Dejar una piedra para el que viene | Cuenta su tramo, ayuda a otro, dona. Así funciona una apacheta real, y así crece la comunidad. |

The last row is the brand's whole community model, and it comes from the real tradition of the
apacheta: whoever passes leaves a stone for whoever comes next.

## Estación 3 — Contenido

### Street rules (read before any street piece)

These come from the medium and from the symbol itself. They protect the brand from looking like
vandalism or like it's using something sacred to sell.

1. **Never touch, add to or brand a real apacheta on a trail.** Apachetas are an Andean offering
   tradition with real cultural and spiritual meaning, and many sit in protected areas. A sticker
   or a plaque on one would be the fastest way to lose the community. "Real apachetas" (D16) means
   **ones we build with permission in places that say yes**: our own events, a bar, a feria, a
   hostel or refugio that agrees, a university hall. ❓ Bruno to confirm that's what he meant.
2. **Ask before sticking.** Stickers and posters in public space and pasacalles are regulated by
   the municipality. ⬜ Check the Municipalidad de Córdoba rules before the first batch. Prefer
   places that invite us (bars, ferias, centros de estudiantes, coworkings).
3. **Every piece leads somewhere concrete:** a QR, one per place and headline (2.7).
4. **One idea per piece.** A sticker has one sentence and the stone. A pasquín can tell the story.
5. **Leave no trace in nature.** In the sierras, the brand shows up in people (a group hike, a
   talk), not in objects.
