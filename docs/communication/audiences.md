# Audiences

Two readers. Same brand, different doors.

## 1. Marcos — the user (inherited from `docs/design/persona.md`)

24, Córdoba capital, studies and works, irregular income, phone-first, WhatsApp. Wants to
make his money grow and beat inflación; needs budgeting and debt fundamentals first; is
overwhelmed, a bit ashamed, impatient. Bounces on anything bank-like, preachy, or homework.

**What we add for him (new):**

- **Perseverance vs. obstinacy.** Marcos has tried an Excel or an app and dropped it in two
  weeks. The real question isn't "will I stick with it" but "is this worth sticking with, and
  how do I know?" Apacheta gives him a **metric he can see** (his balance, his net worth,
  cairns placed) so he can tell progress from stubbornness. *(There's a thin line
  between perseverance and obstinacy: a curve can look flat for two years and then move, but
  you only know it's worth it if you measure something.)*
- **Quieta como una piedra.** The fix for his impatience and anxiety is stillness, not speed:
  one stone at a time, same direction, don't panic on a bad month. The cairn stays.
- **Following the path actually guides to wealth.** The promise isn't a trick, it's order:
  do the boring fundamentals in sequence and the wealth-building part is where you end up.
  He can *see* chapters 4–5 waiting from day one.

**Door:** the landing, the app, the map. Tone: calm, imperative, vos.

## 2. The builder / peer — LinkedIn and blog

Developers, indie builders, people who follow "tools to get traffic", economics and random
useful tools. They don't need to be in debt; they are curious, skeptical of *humo*, and like
seeing how things are built.

**What they want:**
- Honest build-in-public: what worked, what broke, real numbers (once verified).
- Useful tools and methods they can reuse, with sources.
- A person to follow, not a brand page.

**What makes them bounce:** growth-guru tone, vague promises, unverified claims, self-promo
with no substance.

**Why they matter to Apacheta:** early traffic, credibility, feedback, and some of them are
Marcos's older siblings or friends. They also *share* what's useful.

**Door:** Bruno's LinkedIn profile, then the blog and `/herramientas`. Tone: first person,
honest, technical-but-plain. See [channels.md](channels.md).

## 3. Everyone else (same content, less focus)

Argentines who already save and want to invest (UVA, MEP, CEDEARs comparisons) and anyone
landing from search ("cómo salir de deudas"). Served by blog/tool pages written in the brand
voice; no separate persona until we have data.
